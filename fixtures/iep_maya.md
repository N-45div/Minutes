## Special Education and Related Services

The team agrees the student requires the following services to receive a
free appropriate public education (FAPE):

1. **Speech-Language Therapy:** 30 minutes per session, 2 sessions per week,
   provided by a Licensed Speech-Language Pathologist in the therapy room,
   beginning September 8, 2026 and ending June 11, 2027.
2. **Occupational Therapy:** 45 minutes per session, 1 session per week,
   provided by a Licensed Occupational Therapist in the OT room, beginning
   September 8, 2026 and ending June 11, 2027.
3. **Specialized Academic Instruction:** 60 minutes daily, provided by an
   Education Specialist in the resource room, beginning September 8, 2026
   and ending June 11, 2027.
4. **Individual Counseling:** 30 minutes per session, 1 session per month,
   provided by a School Psychologist in the counseling office, beginning
   September 8, 2026 and ending June 11, 2027.

## Reviews and Reporting

- The IEP team will reconvene for the **annual review no later than
  September 1, 2027**.
- A **triennial reevaluation** of eligibility must be completed **no later
  than November 14, 2028**.
- **Progress reports** on all goals will be provided to the parents at each
  grading period: **November 6, 2026; January 29, 2027; April 9, 2027; and

## Program Accommodations

- Scheduled sensory breaks every 45 minutes during instruction.
- A visual daily schedule provided at the start of each school day.
- Extended time (time-and-a-half) on all classroom tests and assessments.
- Preferential seating away from high-traffic areas of the classroom.
